<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="link-slash" title="Wallet won't connect">
    Disable browser extensions that can interfere with wallets (ad blockers, privacy tools). Reload the page and try again. If you have several Neko tabs open, close all but one before reconnecting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="clock" title='Transaction hangs "pending" forever'>
    Open your wallet and check the pending transaction. If the network is busy, your wallet can usually bump the fee to speed it up. If it's been stuck for over an hour, share the transaction hash via [Support](/support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="arrows-rotate" title="Balances look stale">
    Hard refresh the page (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows). If your wallet shows the right balance but Neko does not, it is usually a short indexing delay. Try again in a few minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="lock" title="An asset won't show up after buying">
    Some investments need you to verify your identity with the issuer before you can hold them. Go through the issuer's verification flow, then try again. See [Buying assets](/basic/buying-assets).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="coin" title="It says you don't have enough XLM">
    You need a small amount of **XLM** on the network to pay fees. Top up your wallet and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="network-wired" title="You're on the wrong network">
    Open your wallet and check it's on the same network as Neko. Switch if needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
