USDC is a dollar token. One USDC equals one US dollar. USDC is issued by Circle, the same company that issues USDC on every other major network. This page shows you three ways to get USDC into your wallet today.

<Note>**Coming soon to Neko**: buy USDC with local currency (or a debit card) from inside the Neko app. We are integrating partners that let you fund your wallet without leaving the site. Until then, use one of the options below.</Note>

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- A compatible wallet installed. If not, follow [Install a wallet](/tutorials/install-a-wallet).
- A small amount of XLM in your wallet for network fees. About 5 XLM is enough to start.
- Your wallet's public address ready (it starts with **G** and is long).

## Three ways to get USDC

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Buy on an exchange (easiest)">
    Many major exchanges let you buy USDC and withdraw it to your wallet.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Pick an exchange that supports USDC withdrawals">
        Examples: **Coinbase**, **Kraken**, and **Binance** support USDC withdrawals. Check the exchange's withdrawal page to confirm.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Buy USDC">
        Add money to the exchange (bank transfer or card), then buy USDC.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Withdraw to your wallet">
        Pick **Withdraw**, choose **USDC**, and select the correct network for your wallet. Paste your wallet's public address. Send a small test amount first if you are new to this.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add USDC in your wallet (if needed)">
        Some wallets ask you to accept the USDC asset before it shows up. Lobstr does this with a tap. Freighter does it automatically when you receive.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>Always send a small test amount the first time you withdraw from an exchange to a new wallet. If it arrives, send the rest.</Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Buy XLM and swap to USDC">
    If your exchange does not support direct USDC withdrawals, you can buy XLM instead and swap it inside your wallet.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Buy XLM on any exchange">
        XLM is supported by most large exchanges. Buy the amount of dollars you want to invest, plus a little extra for fees.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Withdraw XLM to your wallet">
        Pick **Withdraw**, choose **XLM**, and paste your wallet's public address. Send a small test amount first.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Swap XLM to USDC inside your wallet">
        Both Freighter and Lobstr have a swap button. Pick **XLM to USDC** and confirm. The swap happens in seconds.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Buy directly inside Lobstr">
    If you are using Lobstr on your phone, you can buy XLM with a card in many countries and swap to USDC without leaving the app.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Lobstr and tap Buy">
        On the home screen, tap **Buy XLM** and follow the steps. Lobstr uses a partner to handle the card payment.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Swap to USDC">
        Once the XLM arrives, tap **Swap** and pick **XLM to USDC**. Confirm.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>Card purchases inside wallets have higher fees than buying on a large exchange. Use this if you want the fastest path.</Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Send USDC to Neko

Once you have USDC in your wallet, open the [Neko app](https://nekoprotocol.xyz), connect your wallet, and you are ready to invest. You do not need to send USDC anywhere; Neko reads what is already in your wallet.

## What is coming next

We are working on letting you fund your wallet from inside Neko with local currency in supported regions. See [Settings: moving money in and out](/app-guide/basic/settings) for the current status.

<Warning>Always double check the address you are sending to. A typo or a wrong network sends the money somewhere you cannot recover it from.</Warning>
