Lobstr is one of the easiest wallets to use on a phone with Neko. It is free, has been around since 2016, and is widely used across Latin America. It also makes it simple to receive USDC and XLM.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    On iPhone, open the App Store and search for **Lobstr**. On Android, open Google Play and search for **Lobstr**. The publisher should be **Lobstr**. Install and open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Tap **Sign Up**. Enter an email and a strong password. Confirm the email Lobstr sends you. This account lets you sync the app across devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back up your recovery phrase">
    In Settings, go to **Security** and then **Backup**. Lobstr will show you a 24 word phrase. Write it on paper. Anyone with this phrase can take all your money. Do not save it as a screenshot, in chat apps, or in cloud notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add USDC to your wallet">
    On the home tab, tap **+** next to **Assets**. Search for **USDC** by Centre. Tap **Add**. This tells your wallet to accept USDC. You will need a tiny bit of XLM first (Lobstr will guide you).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a small amount of XLM for fees">
    Lobstr lets you buy XLM with a card from inside the app, or you can send some from an exchange. About 5 XLM is enough to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Lobstr to Neko">
    Open the [Neko app](https://nekoprotocol.xyz) in your phone's browser, tap **Connect Wallet**, and pick Lobstr. Approve the connection inside the Lobstr app when it asks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Your 24 word recovery phrase is the only way back into your money if you lose your phone or forget your password. Keep it on paper, offline, in a safe place.</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Lobstr safe?">
    Yes. Lobstr is a long-established wallet used by hundreds of thousands of people. Keep your recovery phrase offline and your account password strong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account just to use the wallet?">
    Yes, Lobstr asks for an email and password to let you sync across devices and recover your account. Your money is still controlled by your recovery phrase, not the email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy USDC inside Lobstr?">
    Yes. Lobstr has built in card purchases for XLM in many countries. Once you have XLM, you can swap to USDC inside the app. See [Buy USDC](/tutorials/buy-usdc) for other options.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
