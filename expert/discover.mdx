Expert **Discover** adds the same catalog as Simple mode, plus price feeds, risk settings, strategy detail, and deployment links.

An **oracle** is a feed that publishes asset prices on-chain. Lending and liquidation use those prices. Bad or stale feeds can skew borrow limits and liquidations.

## What you see on each asset

- **Pricing** lists feed IDs (**SEP-40** feeds, **Pyth** on many EVM markets, or hybrids when bridged).
- **Risk** shows max borrow per dollar pledged via **[collateral factor](/concepts/collateral-and-health-factor)** and where **[health](/concepts/collateral-and-health-factor)** can trigger liquidation.
- **Strategy** lists route weights and planned integrations (**Blend**, **Aquarius**, **Soroswap**, **DeFindex**) as the stack evolves.
- **Contracts** link to explorers for bytecode review.

<Tip>Use two windows when you compare two issuers side by side.</Tip>

## When price feeds look wrong

Simple mode cards still rely on the same feeds, even when the UI hides IDs.

If a stable asset drifts far from its peg, contact [Support](/support). Borrow engines may misprice risk until feeds recover.
