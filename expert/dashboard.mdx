Expert mode turns the home screen into one overview.

You see totals by category, positions across earn and borrow, vault summaries, and shortcuts including **Swap**.

If you borrow, you also see cues tied to your **[health factor](/concepts/collateral-and-health-factor)** before a position can close automatically.

## High-level gauges

Top summaries show how your holdings move together. They include yield context when prices exist.

They depend on live prices and feeds.

## Where your capital sits today

The breakdown splits wallet balances, vault deposits, pool positions, and borrow-related balances. That makes skew easy to spot.

Expand rows to compare totals and rounding.

<Tip>For large sizes, spot-check balances on a chain explorer. The UI rounds for readability.</Tip>

## Programs you are running today

Grouped views show lending deposits, borrows, pool positions, receipt tokens where offered, and regional integrations.

Vault sections show strategy names, accrued earnings hints, unstake timers, or banners from integrations.

Shortcut tiles open **Swap**, liquidity surfaces when enabled, ramps, simulator, and other items your build exposes.
