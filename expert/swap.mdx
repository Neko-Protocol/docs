**Swap** is **Expert** only. Slippage, partial fills, and venue risk are easier to mishandle than **Buy** or vault flows.

Neko queries **CoW Swap** on EVM and **Soroswap**, then routes you to whichever quote is better after fees.

## Everyday flow

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Choose sides">
    Pick tokens in the chooser. Only approve assets you trust. Check ticker and issuer in your wallet preview. Copycats exist.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tune execution">
    Tabs switch between immediate-style fills and auction-style flow when the connector exposes both.

    **Slippage** is how far the executed price may move versus the preview. Adjust the cap if you need tighter fills.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Review warnings">
    Large impact or odd previews are worth stopping. Compare with other screens before you sign.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How previews stay aligned

Quotes can differ briefly across chains. The app warns when a preview looks far from other pages.

<Tip>For large trades, compare preview totals with another source before you confirm.</Tip>

<Note>**Swap** stays out of **Simple mode** so newer users stick to **Buy**, **Cart**, and vault flows.</Note>
