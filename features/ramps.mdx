**Ramps** connect bank transfers (**ACH**, wires, or local methods by region) to your wallet.

Partners vary by country. Neko opens the flow your region allows, including identity checks when required.

### Why ramps matter in LATAM

Many users move **local fiat to regulated stablecoin to Neko earnings** in one workflow.

Keep local tax records as your jurisdiction requires.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On-ramp essentials">
    Finish identity steps when asked. Save your receiving address exactly as shown. Keep confirmation IDs if a bank disputes timing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Off-ramp nuances">
    Reviews and cooling periods can slow payouts during fraud spikes. Plan extra time for cash-out.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>Ramp partners change. If a corridor disappears, use [Support](/support). Do not send funds to people who DM you.</Note>
