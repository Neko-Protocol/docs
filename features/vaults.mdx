Vaults are the simplest earn path for many users. You put money in. Balances update with performance. Withdrawals return principal and earnings minus disclosed fees.

On mainnet today, Neko is a live aggregator that routes through **Soroswap** (for trading) and **DeFindex** (for yield vaults). **Blend** (lending) is on the way. **Aquarius** is also referenced as a planned integration.
