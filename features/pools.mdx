**Soroswap** pools are automated markets. Today Neko uses **Soroswap pools** for this surface. You deposit two tokens so traders can exchange against shared liquidity.

Your share is recorded as an **NFT**. You see it in portfolio tables until you withdraw.

## Core interactions

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Add liquidity">
    Pick the pair. Review the price band. Approve transfers. Confirm mint of the position NFT that tracks fees owed to you.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Remove liquidity">
    Redeem part or all of the position. **Impermanent loss** means your tokens can rebalance vs what you would have held outside the pool when prices move apart.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Claim fees">
    Collect trading fees credited to your NFT. Fees still net against fees on the settling network.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>Start small until you see how band choice affects fees vs inventory drift.</Tip>

<Warning>**Impermanent loss** is real divergence risk vs holding each token on its own.</Warning>
