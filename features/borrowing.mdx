You lock **[collateral](/concepts/collateral-and-health-factor)** (assets that back your loan). **[Collateral factor](/concepts/collateral-and-health-factor)** caps how much you can borrow per dollar pledged. Your **[health factor](/concepts/collateral-and-health-factor)** shows how close you are to automatic liquidation.

On mainnet today, borrowing through integrated partners follows the same live-aggregator picture as vaults (see [Vaults](/features/vaults)). **Blend** lending is **upcoming**.

You'll add collateral, borrow, repay, and remove collateral only when your position has enough cushion.

A **health** badge shows green when there is slack, then amber and red as you approach liquidation. A **risk list** highlights positions to watch.
