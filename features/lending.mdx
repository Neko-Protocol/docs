<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an asset to supply">
    Open **Lending**. Compare APY previews when available. Rates move with pool **utilization** (how much of the pool is borrowed).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and deposit">
    Read allowance prompts. Use limited approvals when the UI allows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track receipt balances">
    Supplied balances grow with interest when live. Withdrawals depend on pool liquidity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>**Smart contract risk** remains even with audits. Supply only what you can afford to lose.</Warning>

### Variable APY

Discover and Lending show annualized estimates when connected. Underlying pools reprice as borrowers come and go.

<Tip>Review positions on a set schedule. Dashboards round numbers for display.</Tip>
