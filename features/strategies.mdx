**Strategies** bundle vault and pool mixes into templates with plain-language risk bands, not raw tick maps.

**Use this strategy** opens the guided setup flow. Confirmations save your allocation choices.

Expert users pick strategies when one vault feels too narrow but manual splits across many screens feel heavy.
