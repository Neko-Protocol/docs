## What oracles do

**Oracles** publish asset prices on-chain so programs can set borrow limits, liquidations, and swaps without one offline signer.

## What Neko uses

- **SEP-40** feeds for partner integrations.
- **Pyth Network** streams on many **EVM** deployments used by lending and swaps.

## Why outages matter

Stale, wrong, or paused feeds can misprice borrows or trigger harsh liquidations until operators fix data.

Expert Discover shows feed IDs. Report odd prints via [Support](/support).
