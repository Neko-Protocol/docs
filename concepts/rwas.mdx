## What RWAs are

**Real-world assets (RWAs)** are tokens backed by off-chain income: government bills, rent, invoices, private loans, or similar cash flows with legal documentation.

Their prices tend to track cash flows and macro rates more than short-term social hype.

## Where the yield comes from

Yield comes from real payments (coupon income, rent, loan repayments), not only from trading volume.

Issuers set **identity** rules, redemption timing, and freezes when law requires.

Disclosures live with the issuer and regulators as well as on-chain history.

## How Neko handles compliance

Issuer accounts can set **AUTH_REQUIRED_FLAG**, **AUTH_REVOCABLE_FLAG**, and sometimes **CLAWBACK_ENABLED_FLAG**:

- **`AUTH_REQUIRED_FLAG`**: you finish issuer onboarding before new balances settle.
- **`AUTH_REVOCABLE_FLAG`**: the issuer can revoke authorization for future moves under their terms.
- **`CLAWBACK_ENABLED_FLAG`**: the issuer can recall tokens when their rules and law allow.

Trustlines use **`AUTHORIZED_FLAG`**, **`AUTHORIZED_TO_MAINTAIN_LIABILITIES_FLAG`**, or **`UNAUTHORIZED_FLAG`** for whether you may hold or trade the asset.

Many tokens follow **SEP-41** so wallets treat balances like standard tokens while issuer rules still apply.

Neko reflects these flags in copy. Issuer legal docs hold the full terms.
