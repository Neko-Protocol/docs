## What APY is

**APY** stands for **Annual Percentage Yield**. In plain terms, it answers one question: "if I leave my money here for a year, roughly how much extra would I end up with?" A 5% APY means \$100 held for a year would grow to about \$105, assuming the rate you see today stays the same the whole time.

It's an estimate based on current conditions, not a locked-in promise, the real rate can move up or down as markets change, which is why you'll sometimes see the number shift even though you haven't done anything.

Most products on Neko use a **floating** rate tied to bond coupons, borrower demand, or pool trading fees, unless something is explicitly marketed as fixed.

## Where the number comes from

- **[Vaults](/concepts/vaults).** Income from the underlying strategy: bond coupons, credit spreads, or (for pool-backed strategies) trading fees. See [Vaults](/app-guide/basic/vault).
- **Liquidity pools.** Trading fees, net of **impermanent loss** (the drift that happens when a pool's two token balances rebalance as prices move apart). Pool APY blends fees with that risk, so a high number isn't free money. See [Pools & lending](/app-guide/expert/pools-and-lending).
- **Lending markets** (once Blend ships). A rate that moves with pool utilization, how much of the pool is currently borrowed.

## How Neko shows it

Discover cards and [vault](/concepts/vaults) pages label APY with a timestamp. A fresh print matters most when markets move quickly — an APY from an hour ago in a fast market may already be stale.

<Tip>If you're borrowing against a position, compare the borrow cost to the earn APY on what you're holding — that difference is your real net income, not the headline number alone.</Tip>
