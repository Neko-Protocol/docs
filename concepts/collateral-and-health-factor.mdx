## Collateral

**Collateral** is crypto you lock so a lender will extend credit.

## Collateral factor

**Collateral factor** caps borrow size per dollar pledged (for example **80%** means up to **$0.80** borrowed per **$1** of eligible collateral).

Lower factors leave more buffer for lenders when markets drop.

## Health factor

**Health factor** summarizes how close you are to liquidation:

- **Comfortably above ~1**: extra room before automated sells.
- **Near ~1**: add collateral or repay debt before stress orders run.
- **Below ~1**: partner protocols may liquidate collateral by their rules.

Borrow screens use green, amber, and red badges that mirror contract thresholds.
