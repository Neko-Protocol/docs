## What APY is

**APY** (**Annual Percentage Yield**) states how much income you'd earn in a year **if** today's rate and compounding pattern stayed the same.

Most products use **floating** rates tied to coupons, borrower demand, pool fees, or strategy weights, unless marketing clearly says **fixed**.

## Why it moves

Vault income can come from bills, credit spreads, or trading fees in pools.

**Impermanent loss** happens when pool balances rebalance as prices move; your pool share can lag holding each token on its own.

Pool APY mixes fees with that drift risk.

## How Neko shows it

Discover cards and vault pages label APY with a timestamp. Fresh prints matter when markets jump.

<Tip>If you borrow against assets, compare borrow cost to earn APY so you see net income.</Tip>
