Neko is planning a referral program for community leaders, especially in LATAM. Referrers will earn a share of the protocol fee generated by users they bring in, funded entirely from Neko's revenue, never deducted from a user's yield. Tentative tiers: **1%** under **$10k** referred TVL, **1.5%** from **$10k–$100k**, **2%** above **$100k**. Final terms will be published before launch.

| Approx **referred TVL** bracket | Tentative referrer share\* |
| --- | --- |
| Under $10k | ~1.0% of qualifying protocol revenue slice |
| $10k – $100k | ~1.5% |
| Beyond $100k | ~2.0% |

\*Illustrative only. Signed agreements at launch will apply.
