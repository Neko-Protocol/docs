The **Issuers Portal** aims to help regulated mints onboard tokens: SAC wrapping, **`AUTH_REQUIRED_FLAG`**, **`AUTH_REVOCABLE_FLAG`**, **`CLAWBACK_ENABLED_FLAG`** controls, revocation tooling when courts require it, and dashboards for supply vs reserves.

Retail users would see more approved listings in **Discover** when issuers ship through one pipeline.

Strong issuer tooling reduces freeze surprises and weak disclosures.
