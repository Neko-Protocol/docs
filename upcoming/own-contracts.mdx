<Note>**Not live on mainnet yet.** Today Neko routes through partner contracts only.</Note>

Mainnet uses audited partners: **Soroswap** and **DeFindex** are live in the product surface. **Blend** lending and **Aquarius** are planned integrations. Native Neko bytecode stays on **testnet** until audits complete.

Future **Neko-owned** work targets vault adapters, lending overlays tuned by governance later, and yield pool factories for recurring deposits.

Wide mainnet launch needs third-party audits, economics review, and failover drills.

### Why native contracts matter later

- Fee routing can simplify if Neko controls more of the stack.
- Issuer integrations may move faster when adapters are first-party.
