**Discover** is the catalog. Each card is an investment you can hold long term. You see a short summary, estimated yearly return, and recent price move without raw technical IDs.

## Reading a card quickly

Cards usually show:

- **Name and issuer**: who backs the listing.
- **Estimated yearly return** when the investment earns income. This is today's estimate, not a locked rate.
- **Price direction** for the time range you pick: up or down for that window.

Returns and price change over time. Issuers can update terms.

<Tip>Keep Discover open in one tab and Cart or earn screens in another if you switch often.</Tip>

## Opening an investment

Tap a card to open the detail page:

1. A chart shows price history.
2. Text explains what backs the investment (for example rent or bond income).
3. You see **Buy** or **Add to cart** when you are allowed to trade.

If the issuer needs identity checks, Neko sends you through that flow before Buy unlocks.
