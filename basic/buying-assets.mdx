Buying in Simple mode works like checkout: open an investment, tap **Buy**, review, then confirm in your wallet.

Neko handles the steps behind the screen. You do not stitch anything together by hand.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an investment in Discover">
    Open **Discover**. Tap a card. Open the detail page with the chart and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Buy and enter amount">
    Tap **Buy**. Enter how much you want to spend or receive. Review fees and what you receive. Read any note about identity checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in your wallet">
    When the preview matches what you expect, approve in Freighter or your connected wallet. Check name, amount, and issuer on the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Confirmation time varies by network load. When it completes, your home screen updates your holdings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the issuer asks for verification

Some investments need you to verify who you are **before** tokens settle in your wallet.

Neko links you to the issuer's secure flow. After approval, you can buy and use Cart without repeating checks each day.
