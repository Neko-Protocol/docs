The cart lets you queue several purchases and buy them together in one step. Use it when you want to buy more than one thing at the same time and save on signing each one separately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add from Discover">
    On an investment page, tap **Add to cart** (or the equivalent) before you leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the cart">
    Open **Cart**. Check amounts, names, and fees. Update anything that changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out">
    Tap checkout. Approve the grouped transaction in your wallet when Neko batches the buys.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>**Batch buys can still fail** if conditions change mid-flow. Read the error on screen before you retry.</Warning>

## When Cart helps

Use Cart when you buy several investments in one session.

It also helps when verification is slow. You finish issuer checks once, then place several orders.
