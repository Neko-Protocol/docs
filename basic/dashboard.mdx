## Main stats row

The top row shows totals and recent change in simple wording.

Numbers depend on live prices.

## What you hold today

The holdings list shows investments in your wallet. When Neko can suggest a next step, it shows it here.

You see:

- **Icons and names** for each holding.
- **Amount and approximate USD value** when pricing exists.
- **Short prompts** to open Discover or Cart when they help.

<Tip>Tap a row to open more detail when Discover links are available.</Tip>

## Shortcuts at the top

Shortcut buttons include **Explore**, **Buy**, and **Cart**. Small notices appear when something needs your attention.

They sit near the top so you can jump to earn or buy without digging through menus.
