<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your wallet">
    Install a wallet like [Freighter](https://www.freighter.app/). Your wallet is a free, secure app that holds your money. Save the recovery phrase somewhere safe and offline. Neko cannot recover it for you.

    More detail on [Wallet setup](/get-started/wallet-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Neko and connect">
    Go to the [Neko app](https://nekoprotocol.xyz). Click connect, approve in your wallet, and pick Simple or Full mode. You can change modes any time in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick something and earn">
    Open Discover, find an investment you like (such as a government-bond token), tap Buy, and your money starts earning. Want to see what you would earn first? Use the [Simulator](/features/simulator).
  </Step>
</Steps>
