Neko remembers whether you prefer Simple or Full mode. You can switch any time in Settings.

- **Simple mode** is the everyday way to use Neko, with the technical pieces hidden. You browse investments, use **Cart**, tap **Buy**, watch balances, and use earn flows in plain language.

- **Full mode** is the same app with every control unlocked. You get pooled positions, vault detail, exchanges, borrowing and repay flows, dashboards with [health factor](/concepts/collateral-and-health-factor), and asset pages with price feeds.

## What each mode shows on vault details

| Topic | Simple mode | Full mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you earn | Easy summary | Full breakdown with sources |
| How the investment works | Short, friendly description | Full settings and parameters |
| Safety controls | Hidden behind the scenes | All numbers visible |
| Risk thresholds | Hidden | Visible with exact limits |
| Where the investment lives | Hidden | Full technical info shown |
| Earnings history | Simple chart | Detailed table |

Labels may change as the app updates. **Full mode** shows more risk detail by design.

## When to flip the switch

- Use **Simple mode** when you mainly buy or earn in USD terms and do not need weekly technical detail.

- Use **Full mode** when you manage borrowed positions, pool positions, vault settings, exchanges across venues, or several positions at once.
