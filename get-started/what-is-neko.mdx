## What you can do today

- **Browse investments**: government bonds, real-estate funds, and other tokens backed by real assets.
- **Buy with a tap**: pick what you want, confirm in your wallet, and you own it.
- **Earn while you hold**: many of these tokens pay regular returns, shown right in the app.
- **Try before you commit**: use the simulator to see how much you would earn over time.
- **Move money in and out** through partners that accept local currency, where available.

## Who Neko is for

- People who want to grow their savings in dollars instead of watching inflation eat them.
- Anyone curious about real-world investments but turned off by complicated finance apps.
- People who already use crypto wallets and want a simpler home for their dollar holdings.
