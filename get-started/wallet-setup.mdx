You need a wallet to use Neko. A wallet is a free, secure app that holds your money on the network. We recommend [Freighter](https://www.freighter.app/).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Freighter">
    Go to freighter.app and install the browser extension. Open it and create a new wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery phrase">
    Freighter gives you a 12 or 24 word phrase. Write it on paper and keep it offline. Anyone with this phrase can take your money. Neko cannot recover it for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a small amount of XLM">
    XLM is the network's fee currency. You need a tiny amount to send anything. Buy a few XLM from an exchange and send it to your Freighter address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to Neko">
    Open the [Neko app](https://nekoprotocol.xyz), click connect, and approve in Freighter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If an investment asks you to verify your identity">
    Some regulated investments need you to verify who you are before you can hold them. Follow the issuer's steps, then return to Neko.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>You are responsible for your recovery phrase. Use a paper or metal backup. Do not put it in cloud notes, chat apps, or photos.</Warning>

## Before you confirm a transaction

- Check that the website is the real Neko app.
- If the fee looks unusually high, stop and try again later.
- On a shared computer, disconnect your wallet when you are done.
