Neko is a consumer investing platform, today a web app, with a dedicated mobile app on the way. Think of it like a store. The **inventory** is made up of **[real-world assets](/concepts/rwas)**: government treasury bills, dollar-yield funds, and other tokens backed by real income instead of speculation. The **blockchain** is the **warehouse**, it holds that inventory and moves it instantly, to anyone, anywhere, for a fraction of what traditional finance charges to do the same thing. And **Neko is the store itself**, the place where you browse that inventory, buy what you want with a few taps, and hold it yourself, the same way you'd keep money in any savings app.

<VideoPlaceholder topic="Neko in 60 seconds: what it is and who it's for" duration="~60s" />

Nothing sits in a Neko-controlled account. What you buy goes straight into your own [wallet](/concepts/wallet) and stays there. Neko never takes custody of it, and you can move it out whenever you want. Fees are small and disclosed upfront, and transactions settle in seconds.
