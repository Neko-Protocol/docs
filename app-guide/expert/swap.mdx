**Swap** only appears in **Expert mode** — slippage, partial fills, and venue risk are easier to get wrong here than in a straight [Buy](/app-guide/basic/discover) or [vault](/concepts/vaults) deposit, so Neko keeps newer users on the simpler flows by default.

Neko checks quotes from **Soroswap** and, for EVM-connected pairs, **CoW Swap**, then routes you to whichever is better after fees.

    ## Reading the Swap screen

    Three modes sit at the top: **Swap** (trade right away at the current rate), **Limit**, and **TWAP** (time-weighted average price, splits a trade over time instead of filling it all at once). Below that:

    - **From / To**: pick the token you're spending and the token you want, with your current balance and a **Max** button under each. Tap the swap icon between them to flip the direction.
    - **Amount**: enter how much you want to trade, with the USD value shown underneath. The other side's amount updates automatically based on the current quote.
    - **Platform fee**: the cut Neko takes on the swap, shown as both a percentage and the actual amount in the token you're paying with.
    - **Slippage tolerance**: the most the price can move against you before the trade fails instead of filling at a worse rate.
