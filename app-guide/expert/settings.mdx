Settings holds your mode preference, connected wallet, and other preferences, organized into five tabs: **Preferences**, **App Mode**, **Email**, **Wallet**, and **Network**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Watch a video">
    <VideoPlaceholder topic="Configuring your Neko account" />

    {/* VIDEO_PLACEHOLDER: Configuring your Neko account, 60–90s walkthrough */}
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Read the explanation">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Preferences">
        Set your **Display Currency**, the currency prices and balances are shown in across Neko (USD, MXN, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, BRL, or COP). This only changes how numbers are displayed, it doesn't convert or move any of your actual holdings.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/settings/preferences.png" alt="Preferences tab with the Display Currency dropdown open" />
        </Frame>

        For example, switching **Display Currency** to MXN changes prices across the app, like in [Discover](/app-guide/expert/discover), from USD to MXN (MX$):

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/settings/currency-example.png" alt="Discover showing prices in MXN after changing the display currency" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="App Mode">
        Switch between **Basic App** (simple language, guided experience, hidden complexity) and **Expert App** (full data, advanced metrics, and all protocol features), you can change it here any time.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/settings/app-mode.png" alt="App Mode tab with Basic App and Expert App options" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Email">
        Adding an email is optional, Neko only uses it to reach you about your account activity. Enter an address and tap **Send verification link** to get a magic link confirming you own it, once verified, it shows a green **Verified** badge.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/settings/email.png" alt="Email tab showing a verified email and the change email field" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Wallet">
        Shows which wallet you're connected with (**Provider**) and your wallet's **Address**, shortened with a copy button. Tap **Disconnect Wallet** to sign out, you'll need to reconnect to use Neko again.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/settings/wallet.png" alt="Wallet tab with provider, address, and Disconnect Wallet button" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Network">
        Technical details about the blockchain network Neko connects to: the **Active Network** (Stellar, currently on Mainnet), the **RPC URL** and **Horizon URL** it talks to, and the **Network Passphrase** that identifies this specific network. An **Open Stellar Lab** link takes you to Stellar's own tools if you want to inspect any of this yourself. You won't need to touch this tab for normal use.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/settings/network.png" alt="Network tab with active network, RPC URL, Horizon URL, and network passphrase" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
