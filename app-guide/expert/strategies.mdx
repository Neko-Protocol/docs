## Using a strategy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the catalog">
    Read each card's summary and its past-performance range in the fine print.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a strategy's detail page">
    Compare which [vaults](/concepts/vaults) and pools it includes, and any adjustable settings it exposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personalize or accept defaults">
    Use the defaults, or adjust within the limits the strategy allows, then confirm to save your allocation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>A strategy template saves you time assembling positions, but it doesn't remove the underlying smart-contract risk of the [vaults](/concepts/vaults) and pools it's built from.</Warning>

Expert mode users tend to reach for Strategies when a single [vault](/concepts/vaults) feels too narrow, but manually splitting across several screens feels like more work than it's worth.

## Simulator: preview before you commit

Before depositing into a [vault](/concepts/vaults), pool, or strategy, use the **Simulator** to see an illustrative outcome first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an investment">
    Choose it from Discover context or from the Simulator's own filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Use the amount you'd actually put in — the estimate scales with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare checkpoints">
    Simulator shows illustrative results at 1, 6, and 12 months side by side, so you can see how the estimate compounds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Simulator outputs use today's rates and fees, which can both change. Ask yourself what happens to the projection if rates drop or the market stalls.</Tip>
