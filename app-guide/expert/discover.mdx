**Expert mode** Discover shows the same catalog as Basic mode: the same filters, the same cards, the same price and APY data. The difference shows up when you interact with a card. There's no **Buy Asset** button and no **Shopping Cart**, tapping a card opens that asset's detail page directly.

    ## Same catalog, no cart

    Filter by category (**All**, **Stablecoins**, **Sovereign Bonds**, **Native**) or search by symbol or name, same as [Basic mode](/app-guide/basic/discover). Each card shows the icon, ticker, name, spot price, APY when the asset earns yield, and a category tag. Tap any card to open its detail page.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discover/expert-discover-1.png" alt="Expert mode Discover catalog with filters and asset cards" />
    </Frame>

    ## Asset detail page

    Opening a card takes you to a dedicated page for that asset, with a **Back to Discover** link at the top:

    - **Spot price**: the current price and which feed it comes from (for example "via Reflector").
    - **Price chart**: switch between 3H, 1D, 3D, and 7D ranges, with a **Live** indicator when the feed is streaming.
    - **Overview**: a plain-language description of what the asset is and who issues it.
    - **Details**: further asset-specific data, next to the Overview tab.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discover/expert-discover-2.png" alt="Asset detail page for CETES with price chart, Swap on Neko, and Buy on Etherfuse buttons" />
    </Frame>

    ## Buying an asset

    Instead of Basic mode's in-app buying panel, the detail page gives you two options:

    - **Swap on Neko**: trade for the asset right there using Neko's own routing. See [Swap](/app-guide/expert/swap).
    - **Buy on [Issuer]**: an external link to buy the asset directly from the partner that issues it (for example Etherfuse for CETES).

    <Tip>Returns and prices change constantly, and issuers can update terms. Treat every number as today's estimate, not a locked-in rate.</Tip>
