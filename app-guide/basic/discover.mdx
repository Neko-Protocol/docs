    ## Filter and search

    Filter the catalog by category: **All**, **Stablecoins**, **Sovereign Bonds**, or **Native**, each with a count of how many assets are in it. Or use the search bar to find an asset by symbol or name directly. See [Types of RWAs on Neko](/concepts/types-of-rwas) for what's in each category.

    ## Reading an asset

    Each asset shows:

    - **Icon, ticker, and name**: for example CETES, Mexican Treasury Token.
    - **Price (USD)**: the current price.
    - **APY**: shown next to the price when the asset earns yield (for example ~5.56% APY). Assets that don't earn yield, like a plain stablecoin, won't show one.
    - **Category tag**: Sovereign Bond, Yield Stablecoin, Stablecoin, or Native.

    ## Buying an asset

    Every card has two buttons, and they lead to two different flows:

    - **Buy Asset**: opens that asset's detail page, with its price chart, overview, and a buying panel where you pay with **USDC**, enter an amount, and confirm. Don't know how to get USDC? See our [tutorial](/tutorials/buy-usdc).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discover/discover-2-buy-asset.png" alt="Asset detail page opened from the Buy Asset button" />
    </Frame>

    - **Shopping Cart**: opens a quick buy panel right there on Discover, no need to leave the page. Pay with **USDC**, enter an amount, and tap **Add to cart** to queue it, then check out later with everything else in your [Cart](/app-guide/basic/cart). Don't know how to get USDC? See our [tutorial](/tutorials/buy-usdc).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discover/discover-3-shopping.png" alt="Quick add panel opened from the Shopping Cart button" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>Returns and prices change constantly, and issuers can update terms. Treat every number as today's estimate, not a locked-in rate.</Tip>
