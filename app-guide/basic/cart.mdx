If you want to buy more than one asset in the same session, add each one to **Cart** instead of buying one at a time.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Watch a video">
    <VideoPlaceholder topic="Buying several assets at once with Cart" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Read the explanation">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add an asset">
        Tap the **Shopping Cart** button directly on a card in [Discover](/app-guide/basic/discover) for a quick add without leaving the page, or open the asset's detail page and tap **Add to cart** instead of Buy.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review the cart">
        Open Cart, check amounts, names, and fees for everything queued, and remove or adjust anything that changed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check out">
        Tap **Confirm all**, then approve each purchase in your wallet, one confirmation per asset in the cart.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

   <Warning>If it's the first time you're buying one of the assets in your cart, your wallet will also ask you to add a **trustline** for it before that purchase can go through, just approve it like any other step, then continue confirming the rest. See [Trustlines](/concepts/trustlines) for what that means and why Stellar assets need one.</Warning>
